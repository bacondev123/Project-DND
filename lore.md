# LORE — The Canon of the Meteor Crypt

> The in-universe truth of **DND (Dungeon and dragons)**.
> Where myth and mechanics agree, the rule is quoted in *italics*.

---

## I. The Falling of the Stars

Before the Crypt, there was only the sky's betrayal.

A storm of burning stones fell upon the world — not for a day, but for **forty-five turns**
of the old calendar. *(The ancients counted time in turns; the first legends were stories
of a lone figure, a field of fire, and the discipline to stand still.)* Those who survived
learned to move between the falling stars, and called the art *dodging*.

Where the greatest meteor struck, the earth opened a wound that never closed.
The stone did not cool. It *descended*. Floor beneath floor, the impact carved itself into
a dungeon, and the dungeon remembered how to fall.

They call it the **Meteor Crypt**. It is one hundred floors deep, and it is still falling
upward.

## II. The Delvers

Three orders send their dead-into-the-dark:

- **Fighters** of the Second Wind — veterans who believe armor is a promise and pain is a
  resource. *(High HP/AC; heals itself in combat.)*
- **Rogues** of the Falling Shadow — knife-dancers who strike the way meteors did: suddenly,
  and from the blind side. *(Advantage + extra damage via Sneak Strike.)*
- **Wizards** of the Impact — scholars who keep a splinter of the first meteor and throw it
  back at the dark. *(Firebolt is literally a shard of the falling star.)*

## III. The Laws of the Deep

1. **The Law of Turns.** The Crypt moves only when you move. Time is a staircase.
2. **The Law of Twenty Faces.** Fate is a twenty-sided die; 1 is ruin, 20 is legend.
3. **The Law of the Merchant.** On every floor, before you arrive, he is already there.
   His ledger reads *"time stops while you browse,"* and the Crypt obeys the ledger.
4. **The Law of the Death Cry.** When a dragon dies, something below hears it.
   *(Slaying a Red Dragon guarantees a portal on the next floor.)*
5. **The Law of Greed.** Gold is loud. Carry three hundred coins and the deep leans toward you.
   *(≥300 gold raises portal odds.)*
6. **The Law of the Hundredth Door.** On the hundredth floor, the Queen waits.

## IV. The Dragon Queen

**Tiamat**, five-headed sovereign of chromatic flame, coils upon floor 100. She is the
deepest *fact* of the world — and yet, the sages insist, only a **lid**. Something older
sleeps beneath her hoard; her crown is a seal, not a throne. Slaying her ends an age.
*For now.* *(Temporary final boss; victory "for now… the true Weapons still slumber.")*

The Red Dragons of the deep are her feral children. Their death cry is the only bell
the deep respects.

## V. The Weapons — the secret pantheon

> *Out-of-universe note: the Weapons are an affectionate homage to Final Fantasy VII's
> Weapons. Their secrecy and strength order follow that lore.*

When the star fell, the Planet answered. It forged five colossal guardians to repel the
sky — but the sky went quiet, and the guardians, with nothing left to fight, sank into the
Crypt and dreamed. They are not evil. They are *armed*.

They wait on no road. They appear rarely — a hum, a rune, a `?` — and only to those the
deep finds worthy, or guilty.

- **Sapphire Weapon** — the first sentinel, drowned in blue light; it still guards a sky
  that is no longer there.
- **Diamond Weapon** — the shield that once stood at the world's throat; its hide refracts
  every blow ever aimed at it.
- **Ultima Weapon** — the balance-keeper; the Planet's last argument, written in yellow fire.
- **Ruby Weapon** — the executioner. In the old tongue, *ruby* is the color of a verdict.
  Few have seen it wake; fewer have seen it stop.
- **Emerald Weapon** — the deepest dream; the Planet's final answer, green as the first
  spring after the impact. Some sages whisper that the Crypt's hundred floors are only the
  shell of its sleep.

## VI. The Relics

When a Weapon falls, it leaves a splinter of itself behind:

- **Sapphire Ring** — a cooled tear of the first sentinel. *(+2 AC)*
- **Diamond Aegis** — a scale that refuses. *(−1 damage taken)*
- **Ultima Core** — a still-burning syllable of the last argument. *(+3 attack & damage)*
- **Ruby Heart** — the executioner's verdict, reversed: a pardon, written in blood, for the
  one who won. *(+2 all attributes)*
- **Emerald Harp** — the sound of the Planet dreaming of spring; while you carry it, wounds
  close like winter ending. *(2 HP regen per turn, +1 ability use)*

## VII. The Merchant

No one knows his name. He buys nothing; he sells. Some say he is the last of the old
dodgers — the one who, on turn forty-five of the Falling, simply stopped moving and never
left the field. He will neither confirm nor deny. His ledger, as ever, is closed while you
browse.
